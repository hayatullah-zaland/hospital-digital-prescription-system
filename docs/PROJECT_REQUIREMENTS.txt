Project: Hospital Digital Prescription Management System
I am a university student developing a professional monograph project using the MERN Stack.
I want you to act as an experienced full-stack software engineer and help me design and develop a complete, modern, responsive Digital Prescription Management System for hospitals.
1. Five Primary Project Objectives
The following five objectives are the highest priorities of my project:
1.	Digital Prescription Generation: Doctors must be able to create, manage, and issue structured electronic prescriptions containing patient information, prescribed medicines, dosages, frequency, duration, and medical instructions.
2.	Automatic Drug Interaction Detection: The system must check prescribed medicines for potentially harmful drug interactions and display understandable warnings before the doctor finalizes a prescription.
3.	Patient Medical and Prescription History: The system must securely maintain previous prescriptions, medication information, and relevant medical history so authorized doctors can review them.
4.	Electronic Prescription Transmission: Doctors must be able to send signed prescriptions directly to selected, authorized hospital or external pharmacies. Pharmacists must be able to receive, verify, and process these prescriptions.
5.	Medication Allergy and Sensitivity Alerts: The system must maintain documented patient allergies and sensitivities and warn doctors when recognized medication ingredients may present an allergy risk.
Clinical safety requirement: Drug-interaction and allergy checking must be designed around a reliable, validated medication knowledge source. During academic development, synthetic test data may be used to demonstrate these features, but it must be explicitly labeled as simulated and unsuitable for actual patient care.
